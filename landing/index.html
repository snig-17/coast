The promise
Your money never leaves your phone.

Most finance apps ask you to hand over your bank login and beam every transaction to a server. Coast doesn't. There's no account to create and nothing to sync — the math runs locally, and the data stays with you.

No bank connections No sign-up No servers No ads or tracking Yes — fully open source

Your data, your way

Three honest ways your spending gets in.

Coast will never silently read your bank. Instead it gives you three deliberate ways to bring spending in — you're always in the loop.

1

Tap to log

A quick-add sheet: amount, a category chip, done. Built for the ten seconds after you buy a coffee.

2

Import a CSV

Export a statement from Amex or Revolut and Coast reads it on-device — review each row, skip duplicates, commit the rest.

3

iOS Shortcut

Send a purchase straight into Coast with a coast://add link. It opens pre-filled — you confirm, it never writes silently.

The weekly ritual

End the week with a receipt, not a reckoning.

Every week Coast prints a plain-spoken statement of where your money went. Read it, and stamp it. That's the whole ceremony — a moment of closure instead of dread.
